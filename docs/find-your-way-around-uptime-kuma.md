# Find your way around Uptime Kuma

Uptime Kuma lets you monitor your sites and services, publish public status pages, schedule maintenance windows, and manage application settings. This page orients a new reader to signing in, working from the dashboard, opening individual monitors, and reaching the status page and settings areas.

## Sign in

The Sign in screen is at /dashboard and shows the Uptime Kuma name along with Login and Username fields. This is where you authenticate before reaching the dashboard.

![Screenshot 1](./images/find-your-way-around-uptime-kuma-1.png "asset:cmto72of800009sc8pxgg6mng")

## Quick Stats

Quick Stats at /dashboard is the main dashboard. It displays Uptime Kuma, a New Update indicator, and a Status Pages entry, and it lists your monitored sites so you can open any of them.

![Screenshot 2](./images/find-your-way-around-uptime-kuma-2.png "asset:cmto72psi00019sc8j1tw9x7m")

## Quick Stats

This is the dashboard with its menu open at /dashboard, showing Add New Monitor, a Search monitored sites field, and the Uptime Kuma name. From here you can reach Maintenance and Settings.

![Screenshot 3](./images/find-your-way-around-uptime-kuma-3.png "asset:cmto72vfw00059sc8po2mx010")

## Status Pages

Status Pages at /manage-status-page lists your status pages and shows Uptime Kuma, New Update, and Status Pages labels. Use it to browse existing status pages or create a new one.

![Screenshot 4](./images/find-your-way-around-uptime-kuma-4.png "asset:cmto72wnk00069sc8khdzdb3u")

## Maintenance

Maintenance at /maintenance shows Schedule Maintenance and Learn More alongside the Uptime Kuma name. This is where you plan maintenance windows.

![Screenshot 5](./images/find-your-way-around-uptime-kuma-5.png "asset:cmto72xz400079sc86ds5f8r8")

## Settings

Settings at /settings/general shows Save, Display Timezone, and Allow indexing options. It is the entry point to the full settings area, including Appearance, Notifications, Security, and Backup.

![Screenshot 6](./images/find-your-way-around-uptime-kuma-6.png "asset:cmto72zhy00089sc8qz2g722d")

## Navigation

From Quick Stats, open Status Pages to reach the Status Pages list.

From Quick Stats, open the menu (O) to reveal Add New Monitor, Search, and further navigation.

From the opened dashboard menu, open Maintenance to reach the Maintenance screen.

From the opened dashboard menu, open Settings to reach the general Settings screen.

From Settings, open Appearance to reach the Appearance settings.

From Quick Stats, open the Acme Landing Page live entry to reach its monitor detail view at /dashboard/16.

From the Acme Landing Page live monitor at /dashboard/16, open Recent to reach its Recent view on the same monitor.

## Main tasks

Sign in on the Sign in screen (/dashboard).

Open a monitor from the dashboard list on Quick Stats (/dashboard).

Create a status page on Status Pages (/manage-status-page).

Browse the Status Pages list and open a page on Status Pages (/manage-status-page).

Schedule maintenance on Maintenance (/maintenance).

Configure general settings on Settings (/settings/general).

Switch the interface theme to dark on Settings (/settings/appearance).

Create an HTTP monitor on Add New Monitor (/add).

Read a published status page on Acme Platform Status (/status/acme-platform-status).

Pause and resume a monitor on Acme Landing Page live (/dashboard/16).
